Making data readable

University and internship work where the analysis was only half the job. The other half was getting someone who had never seen the dataset to understand it.

Bridgerton season 3: what the audience actually said

Social Media & Network Analytics, RMIT

A sentiment and network analysis of the conversation around the season's split release. The interesting part wasn't the overall sentiment score, it was where opinion turned and which topics pulled the rest of the conversation with them.

I designed the charts to be read in order, so the argument arrives without needing the write-up next to it.

Tools: R, tidyverse, ggplot2, network analysis
